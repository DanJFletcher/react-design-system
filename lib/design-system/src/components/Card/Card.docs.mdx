## Playground

<Canvas of={CardStories.Playground} />

Use the controls to configure variant, layout, alignment, media placement and crop, size, shape, and heading level. Each card stays a semantic article; its actions remain independent native controls.

## Layouts and variants

<Canvas of={CardStories.LayoutExamples} />
<Canvas of={CardStories.VariantExamples} />

`layout` accepts `vertical` or `horizontal`. Horizontal cards stack on narrow screens and use a media/content split on wider screens. `mediaPosition` controls whether the media appears before or after card content.

`variant` accepts `surface`, `outlined`, `subtle`, or `accent`. `size` controls padding and title scale; `shape` accepts `rounded` or `square`. The component uses semantic theme colors and works with ACME, Atom, Luna, Orchid, and custom themes.

## Content and theme coverage

<Canvas of={CardStories.ContentSlots} />
<Canvas of={CardStories.ThemeCoverage} />

`eyebrow`, `title`, `description`, `metadata`, `headerActions`, `media`, `children`, `footer`, and `actions` are independent React-node slots. `headingLevel` defaults to `2`; choose a level that fits the page outline. If both `footer` and `actions` are supplied, they share the footer row.

`mediaAspectRatio` accepts `auto`, `square`, `landscape`, `wide`, or `portrait`; `mediaFit` accepts `cover` and `contain`. The media slot accepts images, video, or composed content. Provide meaningful alternative text for informative images and hide decorative visuals from assistive technology.

<Canvas of={CardStories.CustomHookOverrides} />
<Canvas of={CardStories.TextOnly} />

Registered `card.*` hooks customize background, foreground, supporting text, borders, corner radius, internal spacing, and media radius. Hooks reset at nested theme boundaries. Native article props and `className` are forwarded. The card itself is not made clickable; place links and buttons in its content or action slots to preserve expected keyboard and focus behavior.

## Accessibility

When a title is supplied, the article is labeled by its heading unless the consumer provides an `aria-label` or `aria-labelledby`. When no title is supplied, the article remains available as an unnamed article unless a label is provided. Action slots preserve their own native semantics and accessible names.
