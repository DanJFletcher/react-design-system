## Behavior and accessibility

- Renders the native `<kbd>` element and forwards its attributes and ref.
- Compose several instances with visible separators to display shortcut chords. `Kbd` is display-only; it does not handle key events or activate commands.
- `size` accepts `sm` or `md` and defaults to `md`. `shape` accepts `square` or `rounded` and defaults to `square`.
- The text inside the keycaps supplies their accessible content; no extra ARIA label is added.
- Uses the shared monospace font, semantic surface/text/border colors, and control elevation across ACME, Luna, Orchid, Atom, Sublime, custom themes, and nested theme boundaries.
- In forced-colors mode, system colors replace the themed surface and border.
