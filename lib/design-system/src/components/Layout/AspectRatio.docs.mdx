## Playground

<Canvas of={AspectRatioStories.Playground} />

`ratio` is a positive finite number representing width divided by height. It defaults to `16 / 9`. `fit` defaults to `cover`; choose `contain` to keep the complete image visible inside the frame.

## Common shapes

<Canvas of={AspectRatioStories.SquareContain} />

<Canvas of={AspectRatioStories.PortraitCrop} />

Direct `img`, `video`, `iframe`, and `picture` children fill the frame. `fit` applies to direct images and videos (including an image inside a direct `picture`); other child content remains in normal flow.

The component renders a native `div`, forwards its native attributes and ref, and adds no surface decoration or ARIA role. Give images meaningful `alt` text (or an empty `alt` for decorative images) and provide an accessible title for embedded frames.
