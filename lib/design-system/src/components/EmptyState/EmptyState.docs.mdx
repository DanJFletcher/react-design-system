## Semantics and content

- Renders a native `<section>` labelled by its title heading. `headingLevel` defaults to `2`; choose another level to fit the surrounding document outline.
- `title` is required. `icon`, `description`, and `actions` are optional consumer-owned slots. Icons are treated as decorative because the title communicates the state.
- `layout` is `compact` by default; use `full` to center content in the parent's available height.
- The component is static presentation: it does not fetch data, change filters, own navigation, or announce itself as a live region. Put search recovery buttons or links in `actions`.
- Pass `aria-labelledby` when the section should use an external accessible name. Native section attributes, refs, `className`, and typed `DesignSystemStyle` are forwarded.
