## Behavior

- Callouts remain in page flow. They are not queued and do not auto-dismiss.
- `tone` is `info` (default), `success`, `warning`, `error`, or `neutral`.
- `title` is optional; `children` provides the message body. `icon` is optional and decorative. `action` accepts consumer-provided links or buttons.
- The outer border matches the tone-specific leading accent by default. A scoped `callout.border` hook can override the outer border independently.
- The component renders a generic `div` without an implicit role or live-region behavior. Set a native `role` such as `status` or `alert` only when dynamically added content needs announcement.
- Icons are hidden from assistive technology; the visible title and body provide meaning.
- `callout.*` hooks customize surface, text, border/accent, radius, padding, and spacing. `callout.accent` controls the leading stripe and default outer-border color; `callout.border` can override the outer border independently. Forced-colors mode retains visible boundaries and text.
- The info tone uses the theme's `color.accent.border` token for its stripe and border, keeping both distinct from the accent fill and the surrounding canvas.
- Theme colors and scoped overrides inherit through nested built-in and custom theme boundaries, including ACME, Atom, Luna, and Orchid.
- Atom adds an 8px-wide decorative halftone strip inside the physical left edge. The dots match the outer-border color, including scoped `callout.border` overrides, while the gaps remain transparent. The strip does not cover the content or intercept pointer events, and is removed in forced-colors mode.
