## Playground

<Canvas of={RequestFormStories.Playground} />

The default quote form collects a name, email, service, and message, with an optional phone field. Supplying `serviceOptions` renders a select; without options, the service field is free text.

## Quote and appointment requests

<Canvas of={RequestFormStories.QuoteSubmission} />

<Canvas of={RequestFormStories.AppointmentRequest} />

Appointment mode adds preferred date and time fields by default. These are preferences only: the component does not check availability or book a slot. The consumer's `onSubmit` callback receives the normalized `RequestFormValues` and the form's native `FormData`, so custom fields can be handled by the consuming application.

## Consent, states, and themes

<Canvas of={RequestFormStories.FailedSubmission} />

<Canvas of={RequestFormStories.ThemeCoverage} />

<Canvas of={RequestFormStories.CustomHooks} />

`onSubmit` may be synchronous or asynchronous. While it is pending, controls are disabled and the submit button shows its loading state. Success and failure messages are announced to assistive technology; editing a field clears the prior message. Native required and email validation remain enabled. No request is sent or stored by the component itself.
