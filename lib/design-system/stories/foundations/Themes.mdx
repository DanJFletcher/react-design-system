<Title>Themes</Title>

ACME is the default for an unthemed document. Luna is a dark, Sailor Moon-inspired theme with midnight-indigo surfaces, moonlight and lavender text, rose-pink actions, crescent-gold accents, and softer control corners. Orchid remains a distinct custom example extending Luna.

<table>
  <thead><tr><th>Theme</th><th>Resolved semantic tokens</th><th>Canvas</th><th>Primary action</th></tr></thead>
  <tbody>
    {Object.entries(manifest.themes).map(([name, tokens]) => (
      <tr key={name}><td>{name}</td><td>{Object.keys(tokens).length}</td><td><code>{String(tokens["color.canvas"])}</code></td><td><code>{String(tokens["color.action.primary.bg"])}</code></td></tr>
    ))}
  </tbody>
</table>

Theme selection is DOM-scoped: `<section data-ds-theme="luna">...</section>`. A nested theme restores its complete defaults rather than inheriting a partial outer theme.
