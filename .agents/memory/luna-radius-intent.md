---
name: Luna radius intent
description: User's intended distinction between Luna surfaces and pill/circular controls.
---

Luna's ideal default surface radius is 32px. Surfaces should remain rectangular with large rounded corners, not become circular. Buttons and form controls such as text inputs and selects keep their intentional pill or circular shapes.

**Why:** The user increased Luna's default radius from 24px to 32px and reiterated that large surface corners should still look square, not circular.

**How to apply:** Distinguish surfaces from action and form controls. Surfaces use the bounded default; non-surface radius roles need an explicit reviewed exception in the design-system radius policy.
